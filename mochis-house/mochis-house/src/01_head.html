Mochi's house

A two-bedroom home with an open living room, kitchen and dining area. Mochi, an orange tabby, wanders between her favourite spots. Open doors, drawers and cupboards, flip the lights, sit down, run a bath, pick things up and play with Mochi, put your shoes on at the rack by the door, dig the garden for treasure, then sell it at the shops across the street.
